# Silk Route launch compliance checklist

The visitor-facing Privacy Notice, Booking Terms, Cookie Notice, Access to Information page and Accessibility Statement are complete and contain no public placeholders. This internal checklist records client-owned statutory and operating details that must be confirmed before the website is published.

## Business identity

- Confirm the full legal name and whether Silk Route is a sole proprietor, partnership, close corporation or company.
- If it is a legal person, confirm its registration number, office bearers and place of registration.
- Confirm the physical business address and an actively monitored business email address.
- Add the confirmed supplier identity details to the website footer and Booking Terms before public launch.

## POPIA and PAIA

- Confirm and register the Information Officer with the Information Regulator.
- Complete the private-body PAIA Manual using the Information Regulator's current template and the client's actual entity, record, contact and Information Officer details.
- Publish the completed PAIA Manual and make the prescribed PAIA forms available through the Access to Information page.
- Confirm the actual hosting, payment, email, backup and other service providers, then update the Privacy and Cookie notices if their processing differs from the current website description.

## Booking operations

- Confirm the commercial cancellation method, payment schedule, deposit requirements, waiting-time treatment and refund process used in quotations.
- Ensure every accepted quotation clearly states the journey-specific price, inclusions, changes and cancellation conditions.
- Have the final legal pages and operating policies reviewed by a qualified South African legal professional before public launch.
