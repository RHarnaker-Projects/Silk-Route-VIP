What is Silk Route?

Silk Route is a private luxury chauffeur service in Cape Town. It provides pre-booked journeys for airport transfers, executive travel, private tours, dinners, events and special occasions.

Which chauffeur services does Silk Route offer?

Silk Route offers daily chauffeur hire, multi-day chauffeur hire, dinner service, and point-to-point or event transfers. Each journey is planned around the collection point, destination, timing and passenger requirements.

Which vehicle does Silk Route use and how many passengers can it carry?

Silk Route's featured vehicle is a Mercedes-Benz V300d Exclusive AMG Line with a black leather cabin and space for up to six passengers.

How much does a Silk Route chauffeur service cost?

Published starting prices are R7,500 for a daily chauffeur service, R7,150 for a multi-day service, R3,950 for a dinner service, and R1,950 for a point-to-point or event transfer. Extensions and optional extras are quoted for the specific journey.

Which optional extras are available?

Optional extras include Wi-Fi, a baby car seat, and an Executive Package with a printer, scanner, copier, shredder and onboard UPS. Availability is confirmed with the booking.

How do I book a Silk Route chauffeur journey?

Use the Book Your Journey form to share the service, date, time, locations, passenger count, luggage and special requirements, or contact Silk Route directly by WhatsApp or telephone on +27 74 537 7310.

Where does Silk Route operate?

Silk Route provides private chauffeur journeys in Cape Town. Collection points, destinations and wider Western Cape itinerary requirements are confirmed for each enquiry.
